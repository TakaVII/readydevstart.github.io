Created: November 25, 2023 8:36 PM

### Indie Game Academy’s Art Careers Guide

> [Join IGA’s Discord!](https://discord.gg/r2Gyv8U3SF)
> 

Game artists are the creative force behind captivating and immersive video game experiences. They transform conceptual visions into tangible digital assets, encompassing character design, environment creation, user interface development, and technical artistry. Through their diverse specializations, game artists breathe life into the virtual realms of gaming, captivating players and transporting them into the heart of the game's story.
