Created: November 25, 2023 8:31 PM

### Indie Game Academy’s Animation Careers Guide

> [Join IGA’s Discord!](https://discord.gg/r2Gyv8U3SF)
> 

An Animator in the gaming industry specializes in creating lifelike movements, expressions, and actions for characters, objects, and environments in games. They use various software and tools to bring game characters to life, ensuring believable performances, realistic physics, and captivating storytelling. Animators work closely with other game development professionals, such as concept artists and programmers, to create visually stunning and immersive experiences for players.
