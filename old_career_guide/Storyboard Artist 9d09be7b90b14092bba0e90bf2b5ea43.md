## Description

A storyboard artist is the visionary bridge between script and screen. They translate the written word into a series of sequential comic-strip-like panels, visually mapping out the narrative flow of a film, animation, or game. Think of them as the director's sketch artist, using their drawing skills and storytelling instincts to capture key scenes, camera angles, character actions, and lighting. 

Their work serves as a crucial blueprint for the entire production, ensuring everyone from animators to cinematographers are on the same page, leading to a cohesive and visually stunning final product.

## Responsibilities

- Transform scripts, game design documents, and gameplay concepts into clear and engaging storyboards that communicate the intended flow and emotion of the game.
- Compose dynamic storyboards for cutscenes, cinematics, and in-game events, using camera angles, framing, and composition to enhance storytelling and emotional impact.
- Closely work with game directors and writers to understand the narrative vision, interpret key moments, and translate them into visually compelling sequences.
- Utilize storyboards to effectively showcase gameplay mechanics, character interactions, and level layouts, ensuring clarity and understanding for other teams.
- Proficiently employ composition, framing, perspective, and cinematic shots to convey narrative beats, character emotions, and action within the game world.
- Vary panel size, layout, and transitions to control the pacing and rhythm of the story, building tension, excitement, and emotional impact.
- Utilize dynamic poses, expressions, and body language to portray character emotions, actions, and reactions within the storyboard sequences.
- Ensure storyboards align with the game's overall visual style, tone, and character designs, contributing to a cohesive visual experience.
- Effectively communicate storyboard ideas and receive feedback from directors, writers, and other teams, adapting and refining storyboards for optimal impact.
