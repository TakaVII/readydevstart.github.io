## Description

Technical animators ensure that characters move realistically and the game runs smoothly, bridging the gap between artistic vision and technological limitations. Their expertise extends beyond animation software, encompassing physics, scripting, and programming to tackle technical challenges and optimize animation tools. 

By collaborating with programmers and animation teams, they develop innovative solutions that push the boundaries of what's possible within the game engine. Technical animators are the architects of efficient animation, ensuring a seamless and immersive experience for players.

## Responsibilities

- Develop and maintain efficient animation pipelines, optimizing workflows and tools for smooth animation creation and integration.
- Integrate physics and simulations, enhancing character interactions and movement realism with physics forces and constraints.
- Optimize animation performance.
- Develop and implement custom animation tools.
- Troubleshoot and solve technical animation issues, identifying and resolving issues related to rigs, animation data, and performance.
- Contribute to game design and storytelling, providing technical insights and feedback to enhance character interactions and gameplay.
