## Description

Financial Specialists are the budget architects and money navigators, ensuring studios spend wisely, maximize revenue, and keep the game financially afloat. 

Their magic bag of tricks includes analyzing budgets, forecasting revenue, securing funding, managing investments, and tracking metrics like a hawk. They negotiate with publishers, optimize spending, and identify new revenue streams, like in-app purchases or esports sponsorships. Ultimately, they ensure the game's financial sustainability, paving the way for creative freedom and long-term success.

## Responsibilities

- Forecast project costs, allocate resources effectively, track expenses, and monitor budget variance to ensure financial viability throughout development and live operations.
- Analyze financial data, prepare revenue forecasts, generate profitability reports, and present clear financial insights to inform decision-making across the studio.
- Evaluate and implement optimal monetization strategies for the game, including in-app purchases, subscriptions, DLCs, and other models, balancing player experience with revenue generation.
- Handle royalty calculations, payments, and contract compliance for intellectual property (IP) licenses, distribution deals, and other agreements.
- Ensure compliance with relevant tax regulations for game development and revenue streams, preparing tax returns and managing tax liabilities.
- Collaborate with game developers, marketing, and product teams to understand product features, pricing models, and potential impacts on financial performance.
- Analyze player purchase data, identify spending patterns, and optimize monetization systems to maximize revenue while maintaining a fair and engaging experience for players.
- Navigate currency fluctuations, handle foreign transactions, and manage currency risks associated with global game distribution and partnerships.
- Participate in financial audits, prepare required documentation, and answer investor inquiries during fundraising or potential acquisitions.
