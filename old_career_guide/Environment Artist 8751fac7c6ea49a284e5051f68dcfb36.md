## Description

An Environment Artist in the game industry is responsible for designing and creating the worlds that players explore. They collaborate with the art director and level designers to understand the game's vision and use their artistic skills to design landscapes, buildings, props, and more. 

They also implement their creations using 3D modeling and texturing tools, considering technical limitations and performance requirements to ensure a visually stunning and optimized game environment. 

## Responsibilities

- Collaborate with the art director and level designers to understand the game's vision and concept.
- Design and create immersive and visually stunning game environments, including landscapes, buildings, and props.
- Use artistic skills to create textures, colors, lighting, and composition to enhance the visual appeal of the game world.
- Implement 3D modeling and texturing techniques to bring the environment designs to life.
- Optimize game environments for performance and technical limitations of the game platform.
- Work with game engines and software tools to implement and integrate environment assets into the game.
- Create and place vegetation, foliage, and other natural elements to enhance realism and immersion.
- Collaborate with other artists and team members to ensure a cohesive and consistent art style throughout the game.
