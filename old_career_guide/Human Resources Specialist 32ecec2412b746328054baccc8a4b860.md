## Description

HR Specialists are the orchestrators of talent and well-being. On one hand, they're the talent scouts, recruiting and nurturing the creative minds that bring games to life. They craft engaging onboarding experiences, cultivate a positive work environment, and ensure fair compensation and benefits. From managing crunch cycles to fostering work-life balance, they prioritize employee satisfaction.

But their role goes beyond just recruitment. They're the confidantes and conflict navigators, resolving issues, mediating disputes, and ensuring legal compliance. They champion diversity and inclusion, building a strong company culture where everyone feels valued and empowered.

## Responsibilities

- Attract, identify, and onboard skilled developers, artists, designers, and other game professionals, understanding the unique skillsets and cultural fit needed for a thriving game studio.
- Design and implement attractive compensation packages and benefits programs that cater to the specific needs and expectations of game industry professionals.
- Champion diversity, equity, and inclusion initiatives within the studio, promoting a healthy and supportive work culture that empowers employees to thrive.
- Address employee concerns, resolve workplace conflicts professionally, and maintain positive relationships between management and staff.
- Ensure accurate and timely payroll processing, manage employee benefits enrollment and administration, and comply with relevant labor laws and regulations.
- Create opportunities for professional growth and skill development within the studio, fostering employee engagement and retention.
- Implement and manage company HR policies, handbooks, and procedures, ensuring compliance with legal requirements and upholding fair and consistent practices.
- Gather and analyze employee data on performance, engagement, retention, and other key metrics, informing strategic HR decisions and talent management initiatives.
