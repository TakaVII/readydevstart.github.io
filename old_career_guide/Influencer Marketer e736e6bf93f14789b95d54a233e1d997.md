## Description

An Influencer Marketer, a more specific version of an Affiliate Marketer (but sometimes with the same responsibilities), is the cool cousin your average marketer wishes they had. They navigate the vibrant world of streamers, YouTubers, and esports stars, leveraging their influence to spread the word about your game. 

They work by crafting impactful campaigns that resonate with specific audiences, partnering with the right influencers, and measuring the buzz across social media and streaming platforms. From sponsored streams to strategic product placements, they turn influencers into living, breathing billboards for your game, adding that extra layer of hype and authenticity, driving downloads and boosting engagement.

## Responsibilities

- Scour various platforms to find and partner with the right influencers whose audience, content style, and values align with the game's target market and overall brand image.
- Craft personalized campaigns that leverage the strengths and reach of chosen influencers, integrating the game organically into their content and engaging their audience authentically.
- Negotiate fair and mutually beneficial agreements with influencers, outlining campaign goals, deliverables, compensation, and reporting requirements.
- Equip influencers with clear brand guidelines, messaging points, and relevant assets like gameplay footage, artwork, and key features to ensure consistent and effective communication.
- Track key metrics like reach, engagement, and conversion rates, analyze influencer content performance, and adjust strategies based on data insights.
- Foster long-term relationships with influencers, maintain open communication, and address their needs throughout the campaign to ensure continued collaboration and mutual success.
- Stay abreast of evolving social media trends and emerging platforms in the gaming community, adapting campaigns and collaborations to leverage new opportunities and reach wider audiences.
- Prepare clear and impactful reports for internal stakeholders, showcasing the reach, engagement, and impact achieved through influencer marketing campaigns.
- Utilize influencers to foster connections with the broader gaming community, engage in fan interaction, and participate in relevant events and conversations.
- Understand and adhere to FTC guidelines on influencer marketing disclosures, ensuring transparency and ethical brand promotion within the gaming space.
