Created: November 25, 2023 8:32 PM

### Indie Game Academy’s Game Design Careers Guide

> [Join IGA’s Discord!](https://discord.gg/r2Gyv8U3SF)
> 

Game designers are the masterminds behind captivating video games, crafting rules, systems, mechanics, and narratives that define virtual worlds. Narrative designers weave compelling storylines and memorable characters, immersing players in the game's world. Game designers collaborate with other professionals to bring their visions to life, iterating and refining designs throughout the development process.
