## Description

A Social Media Specialist is the conductor of the hype train, orchestrating the online conversation around your game. They seek to ignite buzz, foster engagement, and build a loyal following.

This involves juggling multiple platforms, crafting catchy content, running campaigns, and responding to players in real time. They translate game updates into bite-sized stories, celebrate player achievements, and create a vibrant online space where fans can connect. They work as the bridge between the game and its audience, keeping the buzz alive and turning casual players into die-hard enthusiasts.

## Responsibilities

- Develop and manage social media content calendars, creating text posts, images, videos, and live streams that capture player attention and build community engagement.
- Foster positive player interactions on social platforms, respond to comments and messages, host community events, and build a loyal fan base.
- Utilize social media channels to promote new game releases, updates, and special events, driving traffic to the game and maximizing reach.
- Track key metrics like engagement, reach, and sentiment, analyze data to understand audience preferences, and refine content strategies for continuous improvement.
- Continuously learn about new social media features, emerging platforms, and relevant trends in the gaming community, adapting strategies accordingly.
- Work closely with game developers, marketers, and community managers to ensure social media messaging aligns with the overall game vision and marketing goals.
- Address negative feedback and handle online controversies professionally, maintaining a positive brand image and fostering a respectful community.
- Develop and manage paid social media campaigns to reach specific audience segments, drive player acquisition, and increase brand awareness.
- Monitor online conversations for mentions of the game, player sentiment, and competitor activity, providing valuable insights to inform future strategies.
- Explore creative formats, engage in influencer partnerships, and try new social media features to discover new ways to connect with the audience and keep them engaged.
