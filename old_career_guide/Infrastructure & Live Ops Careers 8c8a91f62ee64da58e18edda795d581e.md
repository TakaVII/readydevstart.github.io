LiveOps in the gaming industry pertains to the dynamic management and operation of a live game following its initial launch. It encompasses the consistent provision of support, maintenance, and updates to ensure the game's enduring appeal and player engagement. LiveOps encompasses a diverse set of activities and strategies aimed at enriching the player experience, prolonging the game's lifespan, and driving revenue. Ultimately, the objective of LiveOps is to sustain the game's appeal, captivate players, and ensure long-term profitability.
