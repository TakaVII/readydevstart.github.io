## Description

A character artist in the game industry is responsible for creating and designing the visual appearance of characters in video games. They collaborate with concept artists and game designers to bring characters to life by sculpting, modeling, and texturing them. Character artists use software like ZBrush, Maya, or 3ds Max to create highly detailed 3D models. 

They also work on character rigging and animation, ensuring that the characters move realistically. Additionally, character artists may be involved in creating concept art, creating character sheets, and optimizing character models for real-time rendering in the game engine. Their role is crucial in shaping the overall visual aesthetic and immersion of the game.

## Responsibilities

- Collaborate with concept artists and game designers to develop the visual style and appearance of characters in video games.
- Create highly detailed 3D character models using software like ZBrush, Maya, or 3ds Max.
- Sculpt and texture characters to give them depth, realism, and personality.
- Ensure that character models are optimized for real-time rendering in the game engine.
- Work on character rigging and animation to bring the characters to life.
- Collaborate with the art team to maintain consistent visual aesthetics throughout the game.
- Create concept art and character sheets to guide the development process.
- Iterate and make adjustments based on feedback from the design and art teams.
