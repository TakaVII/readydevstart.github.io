### Indie Game Academy’s Production Careers Guide

> [Join IGA’s Discord!](https://discord.gg/r2Gyv8U3SF)
> 

Production careers in the game industry involve overseeing the development process of video games from concept to completion. Production professionals coordinate and manage various aspects of game development, including scheduling, budgeting, resource allocation, and team coordination. They work closely with designers, artists, programmers, and other team members to ensure that projects are delivered on time and within budget. Production careers require strong organizational and communication skills, as well as the ability to problem-solve and make critical decisions.

### Relevant Degrees/Diplomas

- Bachelor of Fine Arts (BFA) in Game Design/Art
- Bachelor of Arts (BA) in Digital Media/Interactive Media
- Associate of Applied Science (AAS) in Game Art/3D Design
- Diploma in Concept Art/Game Design
- Bachelor of Science (BS) in Computer Science/Software Engineering
- Bachelor of Science (BS) in Game Programming/Development
- Associate of Applied Science (AAS) in Game Development/Programming
- Diploma in Software Development/Game Programming
- Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) in Game Management/Marketing
- Bachelor of Arts (BA) in Business Administration/Project Management
- Associate of Applied Science (AAS) in Game Business/Project Management
- Diploma in Business Administration/Project Management
