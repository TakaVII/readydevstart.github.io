## Description

Foley artists are magicians of sound. They're the ones who bring the aural textures of the game world to life, from the satisfying crunch of footsteps on different surfaces to the clinking of armor and the whoosh of a sword swing. Just like their film counterparts, game Foley artists use everyday objects and their own creative ingenuity to recreate, enhance, or replace sounds that were either poorly captured during development or simply don't exist in the real world. 

They're the alchemist of squeaks and pops, transforming celery stalks into crackling flames, leather jackets into flapping dragon wings, and bowls of cereal into the crunching footsteps of trolls. 

## Responsibilities

- Utilize everyday objects, props, and specialized tools to generate convincing sounds for footsteps, actions, objects, creatures, and environments within the game.
- Layer, filter, and manipulate recorded sounds to achieve the desired sonic texture, enhance detail, and create unique sound effects beyond the limitations of the original source.
- Analyze gameplay mechanics and animation to craft sound effects that accurately reflect in-game actions, reactions, and visual effects.
- Build and maintain a vast library of recorded sounds and Foley props, categorized and readily accessible for future projects.
- Work closely with audio designers and directors to understand the desired sonic vision, interpret concept art and gameplay ideas, and translate them into effective sound effects.
- Be prepared to adjust and improvise sound effects on the fly to accommodate unexpected gameplay scenarios, glitches, or changes in development.
- Consider file size, memory usage, and audio playback performance when creating and implementing sound effects to ensure smooth gameplay.
- Understand the emotional tone and narrative arc of the game, and utilize sound effects to evoke specific emotions, build tension, and enhance player immersion in the story.
