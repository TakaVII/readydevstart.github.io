## Description

Motion Capture Artists are the technical wizards behind the scenes, capturing the nuances of human movement and translating them into the digital world. They are the architects of digital movement, using specialized cameras, sensors, and software to record and manipulate real-world performances for use in video games, movies, and other animated projects.

## Responsibilities

- Set up, operate and maintain motion capture systems.
- Coach actors and guide them during capture sessions.
- Monitor the capture process and troubleshoot technical issues.
- Clean and process raw capture data.
- Create and edit motion capture animation.
- Retarget motion capture data to different characters.
