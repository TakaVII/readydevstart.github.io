# Business Careers

Created: November 25, 2023 8:31 PM

### Indie Game Academy’s Business Careers Guide

> [Join IGA’s Discord!](https://discord.gg/r2Gyv8U3SF)
> 

Business professionals are essential to the success of video game development and publishing, handling strategic, operational, and financial aspects to bring games to life. Product managers lead development, user acquisition specialists attract players, lawyers ensure legal compliance, studio managers oversee operations, financial specialists manage finances, and HR professionals manage employees. Together, they form the backbone of the video game industry.
