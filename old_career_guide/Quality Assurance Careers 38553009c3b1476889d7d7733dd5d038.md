### Indie Game Academy’s Quality Assurance Careers Guide

> [Join IGA’s Discord!](https://discord.gg/r2Gyv8U3SF)
> 

QA professionals play a vital role in testing games for bugs, glitches, and other issues to ensure a smooth and polished gaming experience. They meticulously analyze and report on gameplay mechanics, user interfaces, audiovisual elements, and overall game performance. QA testers collaborate with game developers to identify and resolve issues, verify fixes, and provide valuable feedback for improvements. Their responsibilities also include conducting compatibility testing, multiplayer testing, and ensuring compliance with industry standards.
