## Description

A PR Specialist is a mix of hype squad leader and media whisperer, crafting press releases, pitching stories to journalists, and orchestrating events that put the spotlight on the newest and hottest titles.

Their mission is to build positive buzz and generate excitement for games, shaping public perception and ensuring everyone's talking about the latest masterpiece. This involves crafting compelling messaging, strategically placing interviews with key influencers, and even managing potential crises with a cool head and a knack for storytelling, using their creativity and strategic thinking to paint a picture that draws players in and leaves them itching to experience the adventure for themselves.

## Responsibilities

- Develop compelling press releases, media pitches, and talking points that capture journalist interest and highlight the game's unique features and appeal.
- Cultivate strong relationships with gaming media outlets, influencers, and reviewers, securing opportunities for game exposure through previews, reviews, interviews, and features.
- Proactively prepare for potential PR crises, respond effectively to negative feedback, and maintain a positive brand image for the game during challenging situations.
- Track online conversations about the game, address player concerns, and leverage positive buzz to amplify media coverage and player engagement.
- Plan and manage press events, developer interviews, influencer showcases, and community gatherings to generate excitement and buzz around the game.
- Track media coverage, measure campaign effectiveness, and provide data-driven insights to inform future PR strategies and decisions.
- Work closely with game developers, marketing, community managers, and executives to ensure consistent messaging, seamless event execution, and alignment with overall game goals.
- Understand and comply with relevant legal and ethical regulations regarding game promotion and influencer marketing, ensuring responsible and compliant campaign execution.
- Showcase the impact of PR efforts on key metrics like game awareness, player acquisition, and brand perception, highlighting the strategic role PR plays in the game's success.
