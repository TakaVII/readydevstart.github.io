# Marketing Careers

Created: November 25, 2023 8:32 PM

### Indie Game Academy’s Marketing Careers Guide

> [Join IGA’s Discord!](https://discord.gg/r2Gyv8U3SF)
> 

Marketing roles in the game industry involve promoting and selling video games to the target audience. These professionals utilize various strategies to create brand awareness, engage with the gaming community, and drive sales through market research, campaign development, social media management, influencer collaboration, and analyzing marketing data. Their goal is to position the game effectively, generate interest, and ensure its success in the market.
